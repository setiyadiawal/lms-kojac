## KanjiVG

The Kanji stroke-order viewer fetches per-character SVG data from KanjiVG release `r20250816` through jsDelivr.
KanjiVG provides Japanese kanji stroke shapes, direction and stroke order metadata.

- Project: https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/
